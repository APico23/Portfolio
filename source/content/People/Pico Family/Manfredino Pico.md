The first of the [[Pico Family]]. Took his father, [[Pico De Manfredi]]'s name as his surname, which he passed down to his descendants.
Father of [[Roberto Pico]], [[Ildebrandino Pico]], [[Bernadino Pico]], and [[Guglielmo Pico]].
In 1159, he was registered as a citizen of [[Reggio]].
In 1187, served as the [[Podestà]] of [[Modena]].
In 1198, ceded the court of [[Quarantola]] to the commune of [[Reggio]].  (Which caused great dissension).
In 1202, was elected again as [[Podestà]] of [[Modena]]. During this time, he went to [[Marzaglia]] on behalf of the [[Reggians]] to negotiate peace treaties with the [[Modenese]]. With whom the [[Reggians]] had come to blows with over a disputed claim to the waters of the Secchia.
In 1213, he held in fief a portion of the castles of [[Spilamberto]] and [[Castelvetro]] in the [[Modena]] area, with investitures from the monks of [[Nonantola]], who held direct dominion over them; however, the date on which the house lost those dominions is unknown.
