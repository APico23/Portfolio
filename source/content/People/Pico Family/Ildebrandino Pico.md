Son of [[Manfredino Pico]]. Father of [[Prendiparte Pico]].
In 1188, sworn in as a citizen of Modena.
In 1221, he was included in the confirmation of the court of [[Quarantola]] granted in 1221 by Honorius III to the family of the Sons of Manfredi.
In 1227, he served as a representative of the [[Modenese]] to the [[Bolognese]], alongside [[Gherardino Boschetti]] and [[Leonardo Compagnoni]], to negotiate an agreement that would put an end to the disputes over the ownership of [[Frignano]].
