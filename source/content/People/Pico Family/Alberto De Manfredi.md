Father or brother of [[Pico De Manfredi]].
From the town of [[Borzano]].
In 1179, swore allegiance to the city of [[Modena]]. 
He is mentioned, along with [[Ildebrandino Pico]] and [[Bernadino Pico]], in the first divisions made in 1212 (before the family split into multiple branches).
He is also included in the confirmation of the court of [[Quarantola]] granted in 1221 by Honorius III to the family of the Sons of Manfredi.
