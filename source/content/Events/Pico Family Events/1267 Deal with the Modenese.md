In 1267, the [[Pico Family]], led at the time by [[Bartolomeo Pico]], sold the Castle of [[Mirandola]] and the [[Motta de' Papazzoni]] to the [[Modenese]]. The Picos appeared to have been compelled to make this sale, since the prevailing faction in Modena was the [[Guelph]], and the Picos professed the opposing party- the [[Ghibelline]]. At the same time, they entered into a barter agreement with the [[Prendiparte family]] of [[Bologna]] to avoid losing their properties near [[Modena]]. After that the fortifications of [[Mirandola]] and [[Motta]] were immediately destroyed by the [[Modenese]].
